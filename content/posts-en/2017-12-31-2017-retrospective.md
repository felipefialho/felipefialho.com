If you want, you can follow my retrospectives from 2013 and [2016](/blog/retrospectiva-2016/) (in Portuguese).

This was definitely a year full of professional and personal wins. There were lots of trips, a bunch of concerts, a new apartment, a new job, a hell of a growth spurt for Front-end [BR](https://github.com/frontendbr), among other things.

I'm going to focus only on my professional life, split by quarter. And to keep the read from getting tiring, I'll break it into bullet points again.

I believe 2017 was my best professional moment. I think I'm more well-rounded professionally today than I was at the end of 2016, and a lot of that comes from coming to work at Cubo, surrounded by amazing people and working on several fronts of the project.

Maybe because of that, it was a time of change in my professional positioning. For the first time in my career I stopped focusing so much on Front-end (especially CSS) to get better in other areas, ranging from Back-end development to product planning.

So it was a year of a lot of reflection, and that resulted in less technical texts (and even tweets!) and less participation in open source projects than in other years.

I'm really excited about where things are heading in 2018. Let's all go together on this one!
